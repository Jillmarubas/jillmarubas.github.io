## Description

A Falcon 9 booster falls back from the edge of space and lands standing up on a ship in the ocean. But its engine can't throttle low enough to hover. So it gets exactly one shot. We take the booster apart, from the fuel tanks to the Merlin engine, and work through the one line of maths that brings it down.

About this video
This is an educational, science-explainer animation. The 3D booster is our own model, built as close to the real Falcon 9 as public sources allow (dimensions, tank layout, engines, grid fins and legs). Internal details that aren't public are illustrated. Some numbers are rounded, and the landing example (250 m/s, braking at 2 g) is illustrative. Teardown Lab is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX. The narration voice and the music were generated with AI (ElevenLabs).
