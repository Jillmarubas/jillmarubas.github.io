Shot-by-shot breakdown

Every scene rebuilt to the production file's visual directives (as corrected by the fact-check), in the trading-terminal palette, 24 fps. For each shot: PhotoCraft prep, EffectCraft layer stack and Z, camera keys, on-screen keywords and the sound triggers.

Rules on every shot

  • Photos: three layers from PhotoCraft (plate Z +400, subject Z 0, occlusion Z −250 with 15–20 px of depth-of-field blur).
  • Camera: 35 mm; no static frames: a 4–6 % push per 8–10 s (or a pull-back where the directive asks) and a 0.5–1° Dutch drift through level; camera keys ease at 55 % influence, the Dutch at 50 %.
  • Easing: documents slide in with ease-out cubic inertia (first key 80 % influence, last 0.1 %) plus paper friction; stamps and red badges slam 115 % → 100 % over 2 frames with a desk thump; folders drop with gravity and a 2-frame settle; highlighter wipes take 0.5 s.
  • Finish: film grain 12 % overlay, vignette 18 %, smoke, floating dust motes; one palette; keywords and numbers only (audited by tools/text_audit.py).
  • Sound: a constant 45 Hz rumble (with a 90 Hz layer for phones) under the film at −28 dB, swelling on tense stretches; every visual event carries its Foley on the frame.
