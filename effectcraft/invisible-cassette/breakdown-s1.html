Scene 1: The crash no human could see

Film 00:00–02:04.5 (cold open + title card) · 24 fps · MagnatesMedia-style documentary look · built in EffectCraft 0.18.0 · approved voiceover and mix unchanged

The look

#06070A black#FFB000 amber#19C46B up#FF3B30 down#F2F0EA text
  • One palette from the subject: the trading terminal the crash happened on. Amber phosphor with a soft glow, green only when the market rises, red only when it falls.
  • Type: IBM Plex Sans Condensed Bold for keywords, JetBrains Mono for numbers, symbols and wire text.
  • Kinetic text on keywords and numbers only: slams (128 → 100 % in 5 frames), type-ons, rolling counters with real figures, a break-apart on "evaporated".
  • Depth: terminal grid and dim symbol columns at Z +350, subject at Z 0, big out-of-focus ticker at Z −300 drifting the other way. A 35 mm camera pushes in on every shot.
  • Overlays: drifting smoke (Screen 16 %), film grain (Overlay 12 %), vignette.
  • Brightness follows tension: dark through the plunge, a lift on the recovery, darker into the loop, a snap to black, then the title.
