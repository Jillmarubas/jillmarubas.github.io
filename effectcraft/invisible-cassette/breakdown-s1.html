Global rig (every shot)

  • Camera: 35 mm virtual camera (zoom 1866.67 px), depth of field on, focus locked to Z 0 by expression while it dollies.
  • Motion: continuous push-in of 0.6 % a second (100 → 106 % over 10 s), Dutch drift between ±0.3° and ±1.0°. No static frames.
  • Depth: background plate Z +350 · subject Z 0 (sharp) · foreground occluder Z −250 (out of focus, about 15–20 px).
  • Light: one overhead tungsten spot (warm 1.0/0.80/0.56, cone 78°, feather 100) + ambient 28. Every document: Drop Shadow, distance 18, softness 25, opacity 40 %.
  • Texture: Fractal Noise grain in Overlay at 12 %, CC Vignette 18, CC Particle World dust motes in the lamp light.
  • Physics: documents slide in with an ease-out cubic (fast start, long settle); stamps slam with a 2-frame overshoot 115 % → 100 % plus a 3-frame camera shake.
  • Brightness curve: an exposure layer follows the tension: black open, darker into the plunge, lift on the recovery, colder into the loop, snap to black before the title.
