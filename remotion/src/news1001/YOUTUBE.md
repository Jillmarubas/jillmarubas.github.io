## Accuracy notes
- D "FTC VS AI": the FTC has opened a probe of OpenAI, Anthropic and other labs and plans civil investigative demands. No charges have been filed. Ferguson chairs the FTC; Amodei is Anthropic's CEO and Brockman is OpenAI's president. The title says "opened an investigation", as the sources do.
- E "$355M TAX BREAK": this is the tax saving the NYT reports Meta claimed by counting $4.1B of Zuckerberg's stock-option pay as research spending. The name tag is a visual summary of that report, not a quote. The IRS disputes the claim, and the Tax Court hasn't ruled.
- F "THEY POLICE THEMSELVES": the accord is voluntary self-regulation (Al Jazeera: "accord to self-police"). The six faces are its verified signatories: Amodei, Brockman, Pichai, Zuckerberg, Musk and Huang. Trump hosted the signing. The stamp says VOLUNTARY, not "no enforcement", because Trump pointed to the DOJ and FBI, while critics said it lacks *independent* enforcement.
- Photos are unaltered apart from the background cut-out; nobody's expression has been changed.
