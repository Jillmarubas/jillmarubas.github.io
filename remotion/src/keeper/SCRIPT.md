# The Keeper — beat sheet (wordless paper-cutout short, 60 s, 1080×1920, 23.976 fps)

Built to the `video-production-psychology` and `script-writing` skills.

## Story layer
- **Character:** a girl minding the lighthouse alone while her grandfather is out fishing.
- **Ghost / wound:** implied. The keeper's coat on the hook is too big for her; she is standing in for him.
- **Lie:** "I have to do this alone."  **Want:** relight the big lamp herself.  **Need:** ask for help.
- **Arc:** positive change. She fails alone, rings the bell (asks for help), and the village becomes the light.
- **Schema set up, then broken:** lighthouse films teach us the keeper relights the lamp and saves the ship.
  We set that up, then the lamp fails. The saving light comes from many small lanterns instead.
- **Loops:**
  - Macro: will the little boat get home? (opened 0:00, closed 0:51)
  - Meso: who is on the boat? (opened 0:14 when she watches it, closed 0:53, her grandfather)
  - Meso: can she light the lamp? (opened 0:26, closed 0:31, no)
  - Micro: the rocks the boat can't see (0:07), the bell rope (0:34), what the bell will do (0:35, closed 0:41)
  - Grain of sand: the dead lamp glows once by itself at the end (never explained).
- **Suspense (Hitchcock):** we see the rocks at 0:07; the boat never does.
