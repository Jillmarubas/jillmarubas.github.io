# ColaOrigin: brief (SOP step 1)

- **Topic / message:** how Coca-Cola began, from a pharmacist's tonic to a global drink. The hook question is on screen within 2 s and answered by the end.
- **Length:** 60 s. The user asked for one minute, which overrides the SOP's 14–24 s default. That gives 9 beats of 5.5–9 s, and the last beat carries the call to action.
- **Look:** **white paper** from the user's Pinterest references (breakdowns 1 and 3, plus the first brand-story reference):
  - near-white paper (luma about 245) with a faint square grid;
  - soft window-blind shadows falling across paper *and* objects;
  - soft contact shadows;
  - a giant ghost word behind the subject.
- **Accent colour:** cola brown `#7a2e0e`, used on one word or number at a time. Everything else is ink `#26262a` on paper.
- **Type** (SKILL.md house style):
  - a small tracked-out kicker, then a handwritten lead-in (Caveat) above a heavy italic sans key word (Inter 800);
  - body text in Inter 500;
  - titles at the top of the frame, the hero object in the middle, body copy at the bottom.
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps.
- **Motion:** moves of 11–18 frames, eased in and out; about 55 % of each scene held still for reading.
- **Transitions:** whip up, whip left, defocus, and a warm light-leak flash, each 18 frames and straddling the cut.
- **Assets:** see `assets.tsx`. Dimensions and colours come from the web references cited there.
  - public-domain portraits and nickel scan (Wikimedia Commons / Smithsonian NNC);
  - CC0 Poly Haven props and HDRI;
  - NASA Earth imagery;
  - props modelled in code: the glass, bottle, leaf and kola nut.
  - No Coca-Cola logo, contour bottle or brand red.
- **Sound:** CC0 BigSoundBank recordings, levelled to their body loudness, over a low-passed music bed. Every cue peaks at least 12 dB above the music.
