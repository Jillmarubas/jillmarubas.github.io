# "The Cyclone": sound design cue sheet
Cues follow the script lines (search for the quoted words). The picture will be timed to your final narration file, so exact seconds come later.

**Psychology-skill rules to keep:**
- Sound leads the picture by about half a second at every mood change.
- Keep one dominant sound at a time under the voice, and never mask the narrator.
- Release the tension at the landing with **silence**, then let Oz's colour arrive with birdsong.

| # | At the line… | Sound | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | "But that morning, nothing in Kansas had ever moved at all." | **Cut to near-silence**: dry prairie wind, one fly buzzing | The flashback; flat, empty, gray |
