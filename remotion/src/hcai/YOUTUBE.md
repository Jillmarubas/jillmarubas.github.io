## Title options

## Description
You type a question. Less than a second later, an answer. It feels weightless, but it isn't. We follow the chain behind one AI answer: the power plants, the water, the hidden workers, the rocks and the bill, and settle the argument about what one question really costs.

## Accuracy notes
- Virginia's data-centre tax break: the narration says "hundreds of millions of dollars a year". JLARC put it at $928.6 million in FY2023, and later figures are higher, so the on-screen card shows the JLARC number.
- China's December 2024 export ban to the US is stated as history. An on-screen footnote says it was suspended from November 2025 to November 2026.
- Photos of Montevideo, Memphis, Nairobi and other places show those places. They are not the specific events or people described unless a caption says so.
- The electricity bill, the chat window and the counters marked "illustration" are drawn for the video.
