# Why people hate data centres

A 15-minute explainer, written with the Vox method in `.claude/skills/motion-video/reference/vox-script-method.md`.

- **Draft 1** for approval before production.
- **Narration:** about 1,850 words. At a relaxed Vox pace of 140–150 words per minute, with holds, that comes to about 15–16 minutes.
- **Numbers:** every figure is listed in `RESEARCH.md` with its source. Sources also appear on screen in small type.
