## Description

In 36 minutes on May 6, 2010, nearly a trillion dollars in market value vanished. Then most of it came back. Nobody decided to crash the market. It began with one automated sell order and a feedback loop of trading programs reacting to each other, faster than any human could step in.

This documentary follows that speed from Wall Street to the power grid, the ports and the sky: why grids now rely on protection that acts in fractions of a second, why researchers are training AI agents to run them, why those systems are so hard to explain, and why "keep a human in the loop" is harder than it sounds.
