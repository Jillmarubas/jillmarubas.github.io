How to use

  1. Tap Copy and paste the prompt into your music or sound generator.
  2. Save as WAV at 48 kHz if you can. MP3 at 320 kbps is fine too.
  3. Name each file with its ID, for example F1a.wav, F1b.wav. "Make 3" means three takes, so repeats don't sound identical.
  4. Upload the files in the chat, in any order. Tick Made here to keep track (this only saves on your device).

X1 sub-rumble45 Hz with a 90 Hz layer, because phone speakers can't play 45 Hz. A rough 40 Hz pulse goes in only on tense stretches. X2 Shepard toneEndlessly rising pitch plus a ticking clock, under the plunge (01:00–01:25) and the kill switch (24:00–25:30). It cuts dead at the payoff.

Mix rules I'll follow

  • At most about 5 kinds of sound at once. Under dense narration, only Foley and room tone.
  • Music ducked about 13 dB under the voice. Every hit peaks at least 12 dB above the music.
  • The rumble sits under tense stretches only, not the whole film, so silence still means something.
  • Before each "But…" turn: about 0.3 s of near-silence, then a bass drop-out on the word.
  • Each mood change starts in the sound a beat before the picture changes.
  • Master at −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP.
