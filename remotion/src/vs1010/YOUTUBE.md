💬 Prompts from the video (my examples)
Compare three ways to get from the airport to the city center. Show cost, time and comfort.
Turn these notes into a one-page plan with a title, three goals and a timeline.
Research the best budget laptops for students this year. Compare five, with prices in US dollars, and list your sources.
Make a poster for a bake sale on Saturday. Big title, cupcakes, warm colors, and the text: Bake Sale, Saturday, 10 AM.
Build me a simple tip calculator I can use in my browser. Explain how to open it.
Find every email about my order from last week and tell me which ones need a reply.
Summarize this in ten bullet points. Then list anything that looks like a deadline, a cost or a risk.

5 prompts that work in all three
1. Explain this like I'm new to it, then give me one example from everyday life.
2. Ask me five questions first, then write it.
3. Give me three versions: short, medium and detailed.
4. What's wrong with this, and how would you fix it?
5. Check your answer. What might be wrong or missing?
