## Description

$198 billion of data centres were blocked or delayed across the US in the first half of 2026. More than 800 local groups are fighting them. So why would anyone get this angry at a box full of computers?

In this animated explainer, we follow the story from a quiet street in Manassas, Virginia, to "Data Center Alley", the power grid, the Arizona desert, South Memphis and a Civil War battlefield. We look at who gets the benefits, who pays the costs, and what could make data centres better neighbours.
