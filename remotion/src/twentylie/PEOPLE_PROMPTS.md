# People shots for Google Flow: The $20 Lie

You generate these in Google Flow. I cut them out, place them on the backgrounds and add parallax, depth of field and grain in EffectCraft.

**Rules for every image**
- **16:9 and the highest resolution Flow allows.**
- **Plain, flat, mid-grey background** (#808080) with nothing else in the frame, so I can cut the person out cleanly. I add the room behind them separately.
- **No real people.** Sam Altman is only quoted on screen, never shown.
- **No readable text or logos** on screens or clothes. I add screen text myself.
- **Same look in every image:** documentary photo, 35mm film grain, soft warm key light from the left, muted colours.
