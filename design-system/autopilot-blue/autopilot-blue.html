--ground

The ground is never one grey

The frame reads as flat neutral until you sample it. It holds #EAEAEA across the top two-fifths, then cools through a blue-grey into #CDDDEC at the floor, lit from below right. Every neutral in the system carries that same blue bias — a true 50% grey looks dirty against it.

the page wash — vertical cool-down plus one low bloom at 62% / 118%
--ground-hi#EAEAEATop two-fifths. The resting field.
--ground-mid#DCE3EDWhere the neutral starts turning.
--ground-lo#CDDDECThe floor. Light source lives here.
--surface#F3F4F6Panels sitting on the wash.

The gradient always runs top-to-floor, never corner-to-corner. A diagonal wash breaks the single-light-source read the whole system depends on.

--blue

One object gets the colour

There is exactly one saturated thing in the source frame. This ramp is not a tint scale for decorating many elements — it is four faces of the same object: its body, its lit plane, the rim where a second shape overlaps it, and the bloom it throws onto the floor.

--blue-core#0571F8Body. Fills, primary actions.
--blue-face#2082FDLit plane, gradient origin.
--blue-lift#4D8FFCEdge catching light on overlap.
--blue-veil#81A2F6Bloom on the floor. Never a fill.
--blue-ink#0A50B8Text and links. 6.1:1 on ground.

The one token the source did not have is --blue-ink. That frame never sets blue type, so it never had to clear a contrast bar; #0571F8 on #EAEAEA measures 3.7:1, which carries large display only. Anything at body size drops to #0A50B8.

One saturated object per view. A second one does not read as emphasis — it reads as an interface that lost its nerve.

--type

Type that dissolves downward

The frame stacks five lines and drops each further into the ground until the last is barely a rumour. Instrument Sans takes the display role — a neo-grotesque with the tight apertures and flat terminals the source uses — against Hanken Grotesk for reading and IBM Plex Mono for anything carrying a value.

The veil ramp is decorative only. Below --veil-1, nothing may carry information a reader needs — no labels, no values, no controls. Mark the lower steps aria-hidden and repeat anything that matters at full ink.

--radius

Superellipses, not stadiums

The blue shapes are neither rounded rectangles nor pills. Their corners are superellipse curves — |x/a|ⁿ + |y/b|ⁿ = 1 at roughly n = 4 — so curvature arrives gradually instead of snapping into an arc. Border-radius can only approximate that, and the gap shows most at the largest sizes.

border-radius: 52px

What the browser gives you. The straight edge ends and the arc begins at a visible seam.

superellipse n = 4

Curvature ramps continuously into the corner. This is the shape in the source frame.

The radius scale

Fields take --r-sm, panels --r-md, cards --r-lg, hero objects --r-xl. Controls take --r-pill.

--bloom

Coloured light, not grey shadow

Nothing in the frame casts a neutral shadow. The blue object throws blue back onto the floor — which is the reason the ground cools toward the bottom at all. Elevation here is a tinted bloom with a wide blur and a heavy negative spread, so it stays soft and never draws an outline.

--bloom-sm
0 6px 18px -6px / .35
--bloom-md
0 18px 44px -14px / .45
--bloom-lg
0 34px 80px -22px / .55

Where two blue planes overlap, the upper one takes a 1.5px inset rim of white at 55% on its lit edges. That rim is the only hard line the system permits.

--ease

Everything arrives, nothing departs

The source is a nine-second loop in which a pill widens into a card under a cursor. Its motion is front-loaded and long-tailed: fast out of the gate, then a slow settle. Three curves cover the whole system.

swift cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
morph cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)
settle cubic-bezier(.34, 1.2, .64, 1)
--t-tap 120ms · press feedback --t-move 220ms · fades and colour --t-morph 380ms · shape change --t-stage 640ms · whole-scene

Morph for anything changing size or shape, swift for anything changing place, settle only where a control should feel physical. Overshoot on a card is nausea, not delight.

--components

The frame, made operable

The pill widening into a card is the source motion. Here it is a real control — press it, or focus it and hit Enter. Everything else on this stage inherits the same four faces of blue, the same superellipse corners, the same bloom.
