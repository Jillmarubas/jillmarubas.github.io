House rules

        
    
          
  • Glass sits on the field, never on a flat fill. Over a single color, backdrop blur does nothing you can see.
    • 
          
  • Don't stack glass on glass. A nested blur blurs the blur again, and scrolling slows down.
    • 
          
  • Body text goes on a Sheet. A cobalt fill of 60% or more keeps text at 4.5:1 even over the haze bloom.
    • 
          
  • One stepped headline per screen. The second line indents by 1.12em. That's the reference's signature, so don't dilute it.
    • 
          
  • Animate the glass, not the geometry. Lift, rim and fill change on hover. The blur radius stays put.
    •
