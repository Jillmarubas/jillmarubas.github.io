# Tavern Tales, Episode 1: The Last Lantern of Wren's Hollow

**Format:** a narrated fantasy story told by an old innkeeper beside the fire on a rainy night. Every episode opens and closes in the same tavern (your existing tavern images and clips), and the story itself is told through cinematic stills with slow camera moves.

**Logline:** an old lamplighter keeps a harbor light burning for the son the sea took from him, until the night his granddaughter has to climb the tower alone.

**Length:** about 9–10 minutes (≈1,150 words of narration read slowly, plus music openings and endings).

**Emotional arc:** cozy → wistful → grief → warmth → dread → courage → release → bittersweet twist.

**Twist:** the innkeeper telling the story is Lia, the little girl, now grown old. Her red hood hangs by the tavern door.
