# Tavern Tales, Episode 1: The Last Lantern of Wren's Hollow
## Suno Speech version · 3 characters · 5 chapters

**How this version works:** each chapter is one Suno Speech generation (narrator voice + background music in one track), so the whole episode costs **5 Suno downloads**. You generate the images in Gemini; I time them to the narration, add sound effects, camera moves and crossfades in Remotion.

**Length:** about 9–10 minutes total.

**The 3 characters**
1. **Old Lia**: the innkeeper and the narrator (the only voice you hear).
2. **Tobin Ash**: the old lamplighter.
3. **Young Lia**: his 9-year-old granddaughter (the same person as Old Lia, 60 years earlier; that's the twist).

Everyone else (the villagers, the lost son, the fishermen) is only ever seen from far away, in silhouette or from behind, so no extra faces need to stay consistent.

### CHAPTER 2 · Wren's Hollow (≈2 min 30 s)

**Suno music line:**

```

Grey stone houses clung to the cliffs like gulls' nests...

one old man walked the cliff path... with a lantern pole over his shoulder.

His name was Tobin Ash.
He had a bad leg, a patched grey coat... and hands that shook a little in the cold.
But he never missed a night.
One by one, he lit the lamps along the path...
and last of all... the great lantern at the top of the old harbor tower.

The villagers called him a fool.
"The harbor's been closed for years," they said.
"Who are you lighting it for, old man?"
And Tobin would only smile... and say...
"For whoever is still out there."

What he didn't say was this.
Twenty years before, his son had sailed out on a calm, bright morning.
By afternoon, a storm came out of nowhere...
and his boat never came home.
That night... the harbor lantern had gone dark.
And Tobin had never forgiven himself... or the dark.

But his son left behind a little girl.
Her name was Lia.
She was nine years old, with a red knitted hood, too many freckles...
and a question for everything.

"Grandpa... why does the light have to be on every night?"
"Because," Tobin said...
"the night someone needs it... is never the night you expect."

So Lia began to walk the path with him.
She learned to trim a wick... to shield a match from the wind...
to count the hundred and twelve steps of the tower without losing her breath.
And Tobin... for the first time in years...
found himself laughing on that path.
```

```
The light had never been for one person.

trying to find their way home.
```
