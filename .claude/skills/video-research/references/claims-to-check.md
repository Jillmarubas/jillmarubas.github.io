# Claims to check before quoting

The rules in this skill rest on well-established ideas: cognitive load theory, Mayer's principles,
blend math, alpha handling, the 180° shutter, WCAG 2.3.1, Murch's and Chion's frameworks. Some
specific figures and attributions in the research are weaker. Use them for internal decisions if
helpful, but **don't put them on screen or in a script** without a primary source. This is the
house rule that every on-screen number is verifiable (`motion-video`, non-negotiable 3).

| Claim | Why to check |
|---|---|
| 2026 market stats (91% adoption, 82% ROI, 63% AI use, 89% trust, 85% purchase, 4.8% vs 2.9% conversion) | From vendor marketing blogs (Piktochart, tools8020, Pictory), not independent surveys. Find the original survey (e.g. Wyzowl) first |
| Dropbox: $50k Common Craft video, 10% lift, 10M users, $48M revenue | The video and the lift are widely reported; the user and revenue totals are blog extrapolations |
| Crazy Egg +64%, ~$21k MRR; Rypple +20%; RankWatch +27%; CaseComplete +23%; Jellyfish $800k in 7 days | Agency case-study claims |
| Mayer effect sizes (temporal contiguity 1.30, etc.) | The numbers differ between editions of Mayer's *Multimedia Learning* (e.g. temporal contiguity is reported as about 1.2–1.3). Cite the edition |
| "Sidechain ducking pioneered by Douglas Graham Shearer" | Douglas Shearer was MGM's sound director, but I couldn't confirm he originated ducking. Don't repeat it |
| ~220 ms pre-conscious audio-visual binding | A single line of EEG research; fine as a principle, cite the paper if stated |
| Murch's "2.5 streams per hemisphere" | Murch's own working hypothesis, not a measured brain fact; the practical rule of five stands |
| dotLottie "10x smaller, 70% less memory, 120 fps" | Vendor (LottieFiles / ThorVG) figures |
| FlyU.ai beat-sync tool | Couldn't verify the product; check it exists before recommending it |
| Explainer price ranges (2026) | Market estimates; they vary by region and studio |
