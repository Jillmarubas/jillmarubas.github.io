# 10 topics with demand evidence (2 Nov 2026)

Research method: web search for existing YouTube videos on each topic and their view counts. YouTube, Google Trends,
vidIQ and Social Blade are blocked from this environment, so most view counts come from third-party pages that copy
YouTube's numbers. They are approximate and may be out of date. None of this guarantees views for this channel:
it shows that people already watch these topics in large numbers.

What it can't show (Galloway's outlier test): whether small channels beat their own average on these topics, which
needs each channel's subscriber count. Check that in the YouTube app before committing.

journey), **#3** (data centres look spectacular in 3D) and **#4** (huge proven curiosity, easy to make universal).
